Our story

Three of us. One roaster. Zero chill about coffee.

Singh Beans started in 2020 as a pandemic project between three family members with too much time and very strong opinions. We roast in small batches here in the Lower Mainland, we name our coffees after the places and flavours we grew up with, and we still argue about every single roast profile.
